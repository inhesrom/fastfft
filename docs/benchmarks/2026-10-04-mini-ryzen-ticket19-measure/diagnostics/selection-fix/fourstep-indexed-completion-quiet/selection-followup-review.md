1. Missing stack canonicalization is the best-supported actionable cause. An address-only anchor should make both f32 fused and f64 FourStep local SIMD accesses independent of caller phase and remove their split-line exposure where slots are repacked. If this materially caused false promotions, newly compiled selection should stop approving Small when the public FourStep is faster, and fused14×15/15×14 rankings should transfer more consistently. This predicts reduced context variation, not mandatory recipe choice or universal-address performance.
2. Buffer/coefficient/plan geometry remains plausible, especially the same-recipe165375 spread and remaining variation in already-aligned Small. If the generated stack correction passes yet these failures persist, stack alignment alone was insufficient; retain failure evidence rather than repeatedly rebuilding or changing padding.
